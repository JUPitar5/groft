The Story Behind
Our Kitchen

Founded on the belief that food is the ultimate expression of culture, Groft began as a humble tribute to the vibrant streets and royal kitchens of India. For years, we have meticulously preserved authentic recipes while embracing culinary innovation.

"Our philosophy is simple: source the finest ingredients, trust the slow-cooking process, and serve every meal with the warmth of Indian hospitality."
